# Participation standard

Project spaces are for rigorous, good-faith research and engineering. Participants must communicate professionally, critique claims rather than people, disclose material conflicts, respect confidentiality and security boundaries, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, spam, coordinated manipulation, doxxing, or knowingly false evidence.

Scientific disagreement is welcome. Misrepresenting a forecast as a guarantee, presenting synthetic data as observed fact, hiding a material conflict, falsifying provenance, or using project identity to imply unauthorized endorsement is not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; restrict participation; decline contributions; preserve evidence; and escalate security or legal matters. Enforcement decisions seek proportionality, safety, integrity, and the project’s legitimate interests. Reports may be sent to `info@quebec.ai`; security matters follow `SECURITY.md`.
