Do not place personal, confidential, privileged, or regulated information in public issues, discussions, commits, datasets, workflow logs, artifacts, or forecasts. Public submissions may be copied, indexed, retained, mirrored, and redistributed.

A future hosted service that collects personal information must publish a service-specific privacy notice and implement consent, retention, access, deletion, security, processor, cross-border-transfer, breach-response, and jurisdictional requirements before collection begins.
