# Legal, licensing, and governance implementation

This package reduces avoidable ambiguity; it cannot make any person or organization “fully protected.” Mandatory law, third-party rights, factual disputes, regulatory duties, public-license terms, and litigation risk cannot be eliminated by repository notices.

## Protection layers included

1. **Ownership notice** identifying ENTREPRISE TERMINATOR INTELLIGENCE INC. (MONTREAL.AI) and the principal author.
2. **Path-specific licensing**: AGPL-3.0-or-later for software and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for the paper, prompt, schemas, documentation, and original research artifacts.
3. **Commercial-rights preservation** through a contributor license agreement and a separate-written-license channel.
4. **Trademark reservation** so public licensing is not mistaken for sponsorship, certification, affiliation, or endorsement.
5. **Warranty, liability, reliance, and professional-advice disclaimers**.
6. **Third-party provenance controls** and an express no-relicensing rule.
7. **DCO sign-off plus CLA** for contribution authority, provenance, patent rights, and coherent relicensing.
8. **Security reporting, protected review, CI, release, and immutable-version controls**.
9. **Checksums, release metadata, DOI/preprint procedures, and disclosure records**.
10. **Patent-disclosure warning** because publication can affect filing rights.

## Before first public release

Qualified Quebec/Canadian and publication/IP counsel should review:

- corporate ownership and authority to publish;
- author, employee, contractor, collaborator, university, and sponsor rights;
- patentability, public-disclosure timing, and freedom to operate;
- marks, names, and visual identity;
- third-party software, data, figures, quotations, and benchmark terms;
- privacy, confidentiality, sanctions, export control, and cross-border publication;
- consumer-protection and professional-duty implications of paid forecasts;
- any hosted service, regulated use, certification, support, or commercial license.

## Operational rule

Do not describe a forecast as assured, audited, certified, guaranteed, investment-grade, legally compliant, production-safe, or suitable for a regulated decision unless an appropriately qualified party has actually supplied that assurance under a written engagement.

## Contribution governance

Contributors must sign off commits under the Developer Certificate of Origin and complete the project CLA before merge. Maintainers retain project, roadmap, publication, branding, and release authority. Contributions do not confer equity, ownership, employment, partnership, agency, fiduciary status, governance rights, or trademark rights. Previously granted public-license rights remain governed by their licenses.
