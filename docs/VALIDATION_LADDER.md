# FWAA Validation Ladder

| Level | What is established | What is not established |
|---|---|---|
| L0 Specification | Equations, variables, schemas, and falsification rules are explicit | Correctness or usefulness |
| L1 Mechanical | Tests, builds, manifests, and deterministic runs pass | Real-world predictive validity |
| L2 Controlled | Known synthetic mechanisms are recovered and ablations behave as expected | External validity |
| L3 Historical | Frozen public data support rolling-origin comparison | Prospective calibration |
| L4 Prospective | Registered forecasts are scored after outcomes occur | Universal validity across domains |
| L5 Independent | External teams reproduce artifacts and forecasts | Legal, regulatory, or commercial assurance |

The v2.0.0 package targets L1–L3 and supplies the registry required to accumulate L4 and L5 evidence. It does not mislabel controlled or historical demonstrations as prospective proof.
