# Counsel review checklist

This checklist is an issue-spotting aid, not legal advice and not a substitute for advice based on the actual entity, contributors, users, data, claims, jurisdiction, and deployment.

## Ownership and authority

- Confirm that ENTREPRISE TERMINATOR INTELLIGENCE INC. has written authority to publish every original software, paper, prompt, schema, figure, dataset excerpt, and brand asset.
- Confirm employee, contractor, collaborator, sponsor, university, and prior-employer assignments or waivers where applicable.
- Confirm that the public author and corporate attribution are accurate and authorized.

## Intellectual property

- Review patent-disclosure timing before publication, including Canada, the United States, Europe, and any commercially material jurisdiction.
- Perform a proportionate freedom-to-operate analysis; public disclosure and prior-art notices do not prove non-infringement.
- Review the dual-license map, contributor agreement, DCO process, third-party notices, and commercial relicensing authority.
- Search and assess the proposed names and marks; repository publication does not create complete trademark protection.

## Data, research, and publication

- Verify the license, provenance, permitted transformations, quotation limits, and attribution for each third-party dataset or excerpt.
- Confirm that empirical and synthetic results are separated and that no result is described as independently replicated, peer reviewed, audited, certified, or guaranteed without evidence.
- Review preprint-server and archival-license choices before submission.

## Product and service deployment

- Draft deployment-specific customer terms, privacy terms, data-processing terms, acceptable-use controls, support commitments, service levels, and security allocation.
- Review professional-duty, negligent-misstatement, consumer-protection, competition, advertising, AI-regulation, export-control, sanctions, sectoral, and accessibility requirements.
- Add jurisdiction-specific language for Quebec and Canada, including French-language and privacy obligations where triggered.
- Review insurance, indemnity, limitation-of-liability, dispute-resolution, governing-law, and record-retention provisions.

## High-stakes uses

- Do not rely on repository disclaimers alone for investment, legal, medical, safety-critical, employment, credit, insurance, government, regulated, or fiduciary decisions.
- Require qualified human authority, traceable evidence, documented acceptance criteria, monitoring, incident response, and a deployment-specific risk assessment.

## Release evidence

- Preserve the reviewed commit SHA, signed tag, release artifacts, checksums, attestation, DOI, preprint identifier, contributor records, source ledger, and counsel-approved release memorandum.
- Record any emergency ruleset bypass and the reason, approver, affected commit, and remediation.
