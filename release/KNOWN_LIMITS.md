# Known Limits and Non-Claims

The Ceiling Edition deliberately preserves the following boundaries:

1. The METR and SWE-bench evaluations are compact source-linked historical demonstrations, not economy-wide or prospective validation.
2. The U.S. Census BTOS component is a measurement-comparability audit, not a fitted adoption forecast.
3. Scenario probabilities and discontinuity hazards remain conditional on input assumptions and evidence quality.
4. The generalized realization family adds expressive power and identification burden; it should not replace the hard-min model without evidence.
5. The distributed live prospective registry is empty. Only an explicitly labelled demonstration record is bundled.
6. No DOI, preprint identifier, peer review, independent replication, public repository release, legal opinion, patent right, regulatory approval, or commercial outcome is claimed before it exists.
7. The package cannot guarantee forecast accuracy, legal immunity, freedom to operate, patentability, or business results.
