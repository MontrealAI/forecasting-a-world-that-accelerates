Forecast realized outcomes—not capability in isolation.

Technical possibility becomes consequential only when it survives reliable execution, verification, authority, adoption, and the bottlenecks of the physical and institutional world.

Realized output is the lesser of what technology can reliably produce and what the relevant market, institution, or mission can absorb, after remaining real-world bottlenecks are applied.
